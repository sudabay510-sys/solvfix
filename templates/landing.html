Questions local owners ask

Can you fix my listings automatically?

No. Fix Bot provides claim links and steps. You make the changes, or our done-for-you team can handle agreed manual work with your permission. Only genuine, eligible listings are created or claimed.

Will you create reviews or post replies for me?

Never fake reviews. We help request honest feedback from real customers and draft replies to imported reviews. You review and publish replies yourself. Do not buy reviews, offer incentives, or ask only customers likely to leave positive feedback.

Will this guarantee Google or Maps rankings?

No. Accurate listings support a useful local presence, but rankings depend on many factors. We do not guarantee positions, leads, or revenue.

How long before I see results?

Your audit report is available after the source checks finish; slow providers may take longer or be unavailable. Manual corrections can take days or weeks to verify and appear. Customer response varies, and business results are not guaranteed.

Does “Not listed” prove my listing is missing?

No. It may mean no matching search result was found. Snippets can be incomplete; verify on the platform before making changes or creating a listing.
