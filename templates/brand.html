SolvFix Solutions brand guide

Brand personality

Trustworthy, practical, and on your side. Think of a capable neighbor who's good with tech, not a flashy agency. We're straightforward about what we can and can't do.

Voice

  • Plain English, with short sentences and no jargon.
  • Honest: no ranking guarantees, fake urgency, or made-up numbers.
  • Encouraging. Business owners are busy, so we make the next step obvious.

Color palette

Navy #0B1F3A
Trust, headings

Teal #14B8A6
Action, "fixed"

Mist #E6F7F5
Backgrounds

Amber #F59E0B
Warnings, needs attention

Ink #1F2937
Body text

Fonts

Plus Jakarta Sans for headings, and Inter for body text. Both are free Google Fonts.

Tagline ideas

  • Found. Accurate. Trusted.
  • Fix how the world finds you.
  • Your business, consistent everywhere.
  • Small business, solid footprint.

Logo concept

SolvFix

A wordmark with "Solv" in navy and "Fix" in teal. The icon idea is a map pin whose inner circle becomes a checkmark, showing a listing that's been fixed. It should work in one color and as a favicon (a pin with a check). Avoid gradients in the mark itself.
